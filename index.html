Privacy Policy

Easy Photo PDF Tool (also shown as "HIRA PDF") · Last updated: 19 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Easy Photo PDF Tool mobile application ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this policy before using the App.

In short: your files never leave your device. The App converts, merges, splits and compresses your PDFs entirely on your phone. We do not upload, collect, store or transmit your documents or photos to any server.

Files and documents you select

The App only accesses the specific photos or PDF files you choose through your device's own file and photo picker. It cannot browse or read any other files on your device.

  • All processing (image to PDF, merge, split, compress) happens locally on your device.
  • Your files are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Output files are saved only to the location you pick using your device's save dialog.
  • We do not retain copies of your files. Temporary working files stay in the App's private storage on your device and can be cleared by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or the contents of your documents.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain information, including:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • general device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, and app version);
  • approximate, non-precise location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction data, such as ads shown and tapped.

This information is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which you can read at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn about Google's use of data in partner apps at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can limit personalised advertising at any time from your device settings, under Settings → Google → Ads, by turning on "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" or by resetting your advertising ID.

Permissions the App uses

  • Internet access – used only to load advertisements. It is not used to send your files or documents anywhere.
  • Access to files you choose – granted per file through your device's own picker, strictly limited to the items you select.

The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, call logs or SMS.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data security

Because your documents are processed on your own device and are never transmitted to us, they are not exposed to the risks associated with uploading files to a remote server.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
